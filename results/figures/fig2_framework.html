NEPSE data

  • Daily closing level and turnover
  • 3 Jan 2010 – 4 Aug 2026
  • Source: ShareSansar

Foreign market data

  • 33 equity indices; 27 retained after a coverage screen
  • 3 Jan 2010 – 5 Aug 2026
  • Source: Yahoo Finance

Feature construction (all predictors lagged by at least one day)

NEPSE features (17)

  • Lagged returns, momentum, volatility
  • Distance from moving averages
  • Lagged turnover

Foreign market features (135)

  • Lagged returns (1–3 days)
  • 5-day momentum and volatility
  • For each of 27 markets

Regional features (18)

  • Lagged regional average return
  • 5-day regional momentum
  • 9 regions

Calendar features (2)

  • Day of week
  • Month

Information sets (6)

1. NEPSE-only

2. Foreign-only

3. Combined

4. Global factors (PCA)

5. NEPSE + PCA

6. NEPSE + PLS

Models (8)

Classical machine learning (6)

  • Ordinary least squares (OLS)
  • Ridge regression
  • Random Forest (RF)
  • Extra Trees (ET)
  • Gradient Boosting (GBR)
  • XGBoost (XGB)

Recurrent neural networks (2)

  • Long short-term memory (LSTM)
  • Gated recurrent unit (GRU)
  • 20-day input sequences

Training and evaluation

Chronological split

  • Train: 6 Jan 2010 – 30 Apr 2023 (2,549 days)
  • Test: 2 May 2023 – 4 Aug 2026 (731 days)

Model fitting

  • Imputation and scaling fitted on training data only
  • Ridge penalty chosen by time-series CV; fixed settings for other models
  • Each model fitted once on the training period

Evaluation

  • Hold-out RMSE, MAE, R², directional accuracy
  • Diebold–Mariano and Pesaran–Timmermann tests
  • Walk-forward check (gradient boosting, 6 origins)
